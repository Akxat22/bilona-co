How It Works

Cultured Curd Churning vs Direct Cream Boiling

Unlike commercial dairy ghee produced by boiling raw centrifugal cream, authentic Bilona extracts wholesome butterfat through cultured dahi, maintaining healthy fatty acids and aroma.

1

Curd Fermentation (Dahi Jamana)

Whole pure milk is boiled slowly and allowed to naturally culture overnight into wholesome curd in earthen clay vessels.

2

Two-Way Wooden Mathani Churning

The cultured curd is churned bi-directionally using a wooden mathani. This gentle mechanical motion isolates probiotic makkhan (butter) without heating the delicate fats.

3

Slow Fire Clarification

Separated makkhan is melted on a mild, slow flame until milk solids clarify completely, creating golden, aromatic, and granular (danedar) pure ghee.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our traditional Bilona method, packaging, and delivery.

The Vedic Bilona method is a sacred 5-stage process: raw milk is boiled and naturally cultured into dahi (curd) in earthen pots. The curd is then hand-churned with a two-way wooden mathani (valona) to separate pure makkhan (butter), leaving nutritious buttermilk behind. Finally, the makkhan is slow-cooked on a gentle flame until pure, aromatic, and granular ghee is formed.
Most commercial ghee is produced industrially by separating raw milk cream through mechanical centrifuges and boiling the raw cream directly at high heat. Bilona Ghee is made from cultured curd, preserving natural enzymes, short-chain fatty acids (butyric acid), and giving it an unmistakable homemade aroma and texture without chemical colorants.
Desi Cow Ghee is golden-yellow due to naturally occurring beta-carotene, lighter in consistency, and praised in Ayurveda for daily vitality and children's nutrition. Buffalo Ghee has a snowy white color, a denser grain structure, higher fat percentage, and a richer flavor ideal for festive halwas and active lifestyles.
Store your glass jar in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Do not refrigerate, as condensation introduces moisture. Always use a clean, completely dry wooden or steel spoon. Unopened, pure Bilona ghee retains its quality for 9 to 12 months.
