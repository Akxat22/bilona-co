The Vedic Bilona Method

We don't use high-heat machines or commercial cream boiling. Milk is gently turned into curd in earthen pots, then hand-churned in two opposite directions.

How It Works

Curd Churning vs Direct Cream Boiling

Unlike commercial ghee produced by boiling raw industrial cream, traditional Bilona extracts pure butterfat naturally through cultured curd.

1

Curd Fermentation (Dahi Jamana)

Whole pure milk is boiled slowly and allowed to naturally ferment overnight into curd in clay vessels, activating beneficial gut microbes.

2

Two-Way Wooden Mathani Churning

The curd is churned bi-directionally using a wooden pestle. This gentle mechanical motion isolates fresh Vedic Makkhan (butter) without heating the fats.

3

Slow Fire Clarification

Separated makkhan is melted on a mild cow dung / wood flame until moisture evaporates, leaving behind golden, aromatic, and granular (danedar) ghee.
